CLP(Z)

Integer constraints over interval domains with bounds propagation. Enough for puzzles, scheduling toys and N-queens; residual domains and constraints are shown in answers.

Goals #

GoalMeaning
X in Lo..Hirestrict X to an interval; inf and sup give open bounds; a list on the left restricts every element
A #= Binteger equality of expressions
A #!= Bdisequality
A #< B, A #> B, A #<= B, A #>= Bordering
all_different Xspairwise #!= over a list (all_distinct is a synonym)
label Xsenumerate the variables' finite domains, smallest domain first (labeling is a synonym)

Expressions may use + - * and integer literals; * of two unknowns, /, % and ^ stay residual until they become linear or ground.

Examples #

> X #= 3 + 4?
X = 7
> X #> 3, X #< 6?
X in 4..5
> X in 0..5, Y in 0..5, X + Y #= 10?
X = 5, Y = 5
> X + Y #= 10?
X + Y #= 10
> [X, Y] in 0..20, X + Y #= 10, X - Y #= 2, label [X, Y]?
X = 6, Y = 4
send_more_money [S, E, N, D, M, O, R, Y] <-
  Vars = [S, E, N, D, M, O, R, Y],
  Vars in 0..9,
  all_different Vars,
  S #!= 0, M #!= 0,
  1000 * S + 100 * E + 10 * N + D + 1000 * M + 100 * O + 10 * R + E
    #= 10000 * M + 1000 * O + 100 * N + 10 * E + Y,
  label Vars.

> send_more_money L?
L = [9, 5, 6, 7, 1, 0, 8, 2]

How propagation works #

  • Domains are intervals with a set of excluded values. Unifying a constrained variable with an integer checks the domain; with another variable, the domains are intersected; with anything else it is a type error.
  • Linear constraints Σ cᵢxᵢ + k ⋈ 0 get bounds propagation after every unification and every new constraint, to a fixpoint. A constraint over a single variable is absorbed into its domain; a domain that shrinks to one value binds the variable.
  • #!= with one unknown punches a hole in its domain; with several it waits.
  • Labeling picks the unbound variable with the smallest finite domain and tries its values in order, propagating after each. Labeling a variable with an unbounded domain is an error.

The general disequality A != B is also a delayed constraint (it is checked whenever its variables get bound) and appears in answers as X != 1.
